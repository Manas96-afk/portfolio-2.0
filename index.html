Experience the 3D Corridor: A Journey Through Motion and Code

Welcome to the Manas Bandhu Digital Universe

Welcome to the interactive 3D portfolio of Manas Bandhu, widely recognized in the digital space as @cnomo_editz. If you are searching for a creative video editor, a skilled motion designer, or a web developer who treats the browser as a canvas, you have arrived at the right place. This website is not just a standard resume; it is an immersive, fully interactive WebGL experience. When you enter this portfolio, you find yourself in a carefully hand-drawn 3D corridor. Every texture, every frame, and every interactive element was custom-crafted to demonstrate what happens when cinematic editing meets modern web technology.

My goal is to bridge the gap between static web pages and cinematic, game-like experiences running natively in the browser. Built with React Three Fiber, Three.js, and GSAP, this 3D corridor runs smoothly across devices, maintaining high performance without sacrificing visual fidelity. The corridor serves as the central hub, with doors leading to different aspects of my work: my projects, my video edits, my story, and my contact information.

About Manas Bandhu: The Editor and Developer Behind the Screen

My Creative Journey

With over 4 years of experience, I have produced 150+ videos that have generated more than 10 million views, alongside shipping 25+ web projects. I operate at the intersection of rhythm, motion, and clean code — crafting high-impact cinematic visual stories and experimental digital experiences.

The Gallery: Selected Works

In the 3D gallery room, you can walk through an exhibition of my most significant projects. Here is a breakdown of the work created by Manas Bandhu.

Rasmalai — AI Desktop Assistant

Rasmalai is an AI desktop assistant built with Python and Gemini Flash. It handles voice and vision commands, runs quietly in the system tray, and acts as a lightweight everyday companion on your computer — a hands-on exploration of practical, local-first AI.

DrugDex — Healthcare Platform

DrugDex is a healthcare platform with a Node and Express REST API. It ships 11 REST endpoints and a fuzzy search engine for fast, forgiving medicine lookups, built to be reliable and easy to extend.

Manas — Personal Website

My personal portfolio site, built with TypeScript and React, styled with hand-tuned CSS and deployed on Vercel. It is a clean, fast home for my work and writing — the same foundation this 3D experience builds upon.
